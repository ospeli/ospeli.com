Revenue cycle: from registration to payment

For organisations that bill insurers, other payers or patients. The front desk checks coverage and authorisation for today's arrivals. The billing officer sees what is ready to bill, what is missing, and why claims were denied. The finance lead sees how long claims take and what is still owed. The guide suggests; a person decides.

Optional module, switched off by default. Only organisations that bill turn it on. Everyone else never sees billing screens or coverage questions, and the rest of Ospeli works the same. It never ranks, delays or changes care by coverage or ability to pay, and it does not assign clinical codes.

What happened today

    Guide

    The guide reads the encounters, claims and denials and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. A person confirms it.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its patients (shown only as anonymous codes), payer groups, claims and amounts (in currency units). Rules such as "appeal within 7 days" are example settings, not a standard. It measures claims and steps, never individual staff.
