Maternity and children's flow: rooms, cots, beds and transfers

One live board for labour rooms, postnatal beds, neonatal cots and children's beds. Each shows whether it is occupied, ready, being cleaned or closed. Admissions, discharges, transfers and unit closures are logged with times and reasons, so nobody has to phone round. Clinical teams make every clinical decision; Ospeli records what was decided and shows the flow.

    Guide

    The guide reads place states, expected admissions, transfer waits, closures and your settings, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. It never uses anything about a woman's, baby's or child's condition. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal; a person decides.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its units, partner sites, codes and times. Women, babies and children appear only as anonymous codes; a baby's code is linked to the mother's so they move together. Ospeli does not assess labour, decide transfers on clinical grounds or prioritise women, babies or children, and never scores midwives or nurses. Coming next (Jan 2027): the full maternity and children's board across sites, mother and baby codes linked across every move, and the capacity review and transfer walk playbooks.
