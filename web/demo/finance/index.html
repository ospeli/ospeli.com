Finance and budgets: spend beside the work behind it

Each ward and department sees its budget, what it spent and why, the week the month closes. Spend sits beside activity from the event log, so cost per occupied bed-day uses the same counts as operations. Budget holders explain variances in a line; finance sees what is still missing; leadership sees which costs follow the beds and theatre lists. The guide suggests; a person decides.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the month's ledger lines, the budget, the tolerance and the activity counts, and suggests where to look first, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. It can draft a variance note, but the budget holder edits and sends it. Other suggestions only become proposals; the finance partner decides.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its cost centres, budgets, spend, activity and notes. Amounts are in generic currency units. It measures spend and activity by cost centre, never individual staff or patients' care. It is not an accounting ledger or payroll system. Coming next (Feb 2027): ledger extract import with the source file kept, cost centre mapping, spend requests with approval, budget setting with manager sign-off, and recovery plans tracked against budget.
