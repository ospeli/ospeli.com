What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads today's clinics, the waiting list and the free slots, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal; a person confirms it. It uses waiting times and slot data only, never clinical urgency.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its services, clinics, rooms, codes and times. Patients appear only as anonymous codes. Targets are example settings that each service sets for itself, not national standards. It measures clinics and waits, never individual clinicians, and it does not decide the clinical urgency of a referral or who needs follow-up. Coming next (Jan 2027): clinic templates with held and released slots, referral intake that starts the waiting-time clock, reminder replies linked to attendance, and a clinic day summary for the operations manager.
