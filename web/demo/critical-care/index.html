Critical care capacity: beds, step-downs and transfers out

One live board for the critical care unit. Every bed shows its state and whether it is staffed. Patients the team has decided are ready to step down are timed until they leave for a ward bed. Referrals refused and transfers out for lack of capacity are counted, day and night. The clinical team decides who is admitted or stepped down; Ospeli records it and shows the flow.

    Guide

    The guide reads the bed states, step-down waits, referrals and your settings, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. It never uses anything about a patient's condition. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal; a person decides.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its critical care unit, wards, patient codes and times. Patients appear only as anonymous codes. Ospeli does not decide admission to, or readiness to leave, critical care, and never scores clinicians or units.
