Stores and stock

See what each area has on the shelf, what is running out and where each order has got to. Ward staff report and request on a phone. The storekeeper issues, receives and counts. The supply manager sees the gaps. The guide suggests reorders and level changes with its reasons; a person decides.

Example General Hospital

Guide

The guide reads the stock, requests and orders and suggests what to look at, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. It only proposes: orders, level changes and transfers happen when a person confirms them, and purchases are approved by people in the purchasing process.

Today's log

    Items, quantities, usage and orders are invented for this demo. Tracer items are a short local list of items that must never run out; the settings used here (4 hours for “on time”, 1.5 days of use for a top-up level, 7 days for delivery) are example settings an organisation would choose for itself.
