What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads checklists, drills, actions and the incident card, and points out gaps, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Thresholds are example settings, not a standard. A suggestion only becomes a proposal; a person decides.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its areas, contacts, stock and drills. It tracks plans, checklists, drills and actions, never individual staff performance. It does not triage casualties or set clinical priorities in an emergency; clinicians decide care. Coming next (Mar 2027): the WHO Hospital Safety Index self-assessment, generator, fuel and water checks on a phone during rounds, drill reviews drafted from the timed log, and a downtime pack built from live bed, staff and stock data.
