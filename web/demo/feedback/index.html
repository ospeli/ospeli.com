Patient feedback and complaints

Patients and visitors leave a three-question card on their phone. Complaints and compliments from any channel get one record, an owner role and due dates. Department leads see the themes for their area and publish what changed. The guide flags complaints near their due date and drafts proposals; a person decides.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the comments, complaints, due dates and actions, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal or a draft. A person confirms, edits and publishes.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its departments, comments, complaints and dates. Response deadlines are example settings, set locally, not a standard. Themes describe services and processes; it never names, scores or ranks staff, and it does not judge clinical care: clinical concerns go to clinical review. Patients are known only by a reference code. Coming next (Dec 2026): log complaints from letters, email, phone and the desk in one place, full response timelines, a themes board with control-chart trends, and a monthly feedback report.
