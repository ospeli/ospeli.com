What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the roster, open shifts, workload counts and the settings your site chose, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. The unit manager decides.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its units, the staff initials, rosters and workload counts. It counts cover by unit, shift and skill group; it never scores, ranks or compares people, and it has nothing to do with pay. Staff needed comes from a workload basis each site sets for itself; the numbers here are example settings, not a standard, and the demo does not set clinical acuity or care decisions. Coming next (Feb 2027): roster planning weeks ahead with leave and minimum cover, cover requests tracked from gap to filled, a full credentials tracker for HR, and staff needed against staff available over time, by unit.
