Public health operations: outreach sessions, stock and cold chain

Vaccination outreach and screening days run from one log. Outreach teams check people in, count stock used and log cold box readings on a phone that keeps working without a signal. The coordinator sees which sessions lack a team, transport or stock, what each site holds and when it expires, and cold chain alarms as they happen. The programme manager sees sessions held, people reached and wastage. The guide points things out; a person decides.

    Guide

    The guide reads sessions, stock, expiry dates and cold chain readings, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. The campaign coordinator confirms or declines it.

    Everything here is invented: the outreach programme of Example General Hospital, its health centres, venues, teams, vehicles, stock and readings. It counts sessions, people checked in, stock and temperatures, never individual staff. It does not track cases or contacts, decide who should be vaccinated or screened, or judge whether stock is safe to use after an excursion; those decisions stay with the responsible people. Coming in Mar 2027: campaign plans, session check-ins, stock by site and cold chain readings sharing one log, with transfers between sites and work orders for faulty fridges.
