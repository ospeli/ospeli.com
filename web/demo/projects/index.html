Planning and capital projects

From idea to benefits in one place. Project leads update milestones, risks and spend from a phone. The programme office sees every project by stage, with its status explained. Leadership sees the portfolio, the decisions waiting, and whether promised benefits came. The guide points out what needs attention; people decide.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads milestones, decisions, benefits and the draft business case, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. A person confirms it, and leadership makes every investment decision.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its projects, amounts, dates and measures. Amounts are in thousands of currency units (k) and no currency is assumed. Status colours and thresholds are example settings, not a standard. It tracks projects and measures, never individual staff, and it does not make investment decisions or rank services by clinical value; leadership decides. Coming next (Feb 2027): business cases that draw on the site's own time stamps, process maps and costs; a redesign workspace with current and future maps, layout options and capacity checks; photo notes from site walks; and benefits reviews at set points after go-live.
