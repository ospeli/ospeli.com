Guide

The guide reads this report and suggests what to discuss at the morning huddle, each with its reason. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. It only proposes: a person edits the huddle list and decides.

All names, units, numbers and dates here are invented for this demo and do not describe any real place.
