Facilities and utilities: faults, planned work and plant checks

Anyone can report a leak, power cut, broken light, air conditioning, lift or door fault from the room in three taps. Technicians work the queue on a phone. The facilities manager sees open work orders, planned maintenance, utility checks and which plant is up or down. The guide points out patterns; a person decides.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads open work orders, planned jobs and the utility log, and points out patterns with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. The facilities manager decides. Ospeli never switches or controls plant.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its buildings, rooms, plant, jobs and times. It measures jobs, checks and outages, never individual technicians. It is not a building management or control system and never switches plant. Coming next (Dec 2026): QR labels for every room and plant item, the compliance calendar for fire, water safety, lift, pressure system and gas inspections with certificates, and the healthcare waste log by stream.
