Emergency department flow

Every step from arrival to leaving the department gets a time, so the team can see who is waiting, for what, and where the day slows down. Pick a role to see its view. Ospeli records times and suggests operational actions; people decide, and clinical decisions stay with the care team.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the board and suggests operational actions, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Buttons only propose a change: a person confirms it. It never suggests triage, clinical priorities or diagnoses.

    Patients, times, wards and thresholds are invented for this demo. Pressure thresholds and action cards are set by each site; the ones here are examples. Coming next (January 2027): emergency department flow in the app, on the same event log as the bed board, with ambulance arrival and handover times and pressure-level history.
