Support tasks: cleaning, portering, linen and meals

Bed cleans, patient moves, specimens, linen and meal deliveries become tracked jobs. Porters and cleaners take them on a phone. The supervisor sees every open job and which teams are free. Wards see when help is coming. The guide flags waits past your targets; a person decides.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the open jobs, team numbers and your targets, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. The supervisor decides.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its wards, teams, jobs and times. It measures jobs and waits, never individual cleaners or porters, and it does not set isolation or infection decisions or patient diets. Coming next (Dec 2026): cleaning audits on a phone by room and risk category, linen stock against top-up levels, meal deliveries and returns, and the live link from discharges on the bed board.
