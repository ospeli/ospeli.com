Mental health and long-stay beds: delayed transfers and placements

Patients can stay for weeks after they are ready to leave, while a placement, housing or funding is arranged. Here each ward records the ready-to-leave date and the reason in a few taps. The coordinator sees every delay and where placement requests wait. Operations sees the bed-days lost and which causes are growing. The guide points at the longest waits; a person decides.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the delays, placement stages, free beds and out-of-area counts, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. It does not judge readiness to leave, risk or treatment. A suggestion only becomes a proposal; the named role decides.

    Guide settings (example settings)

    Example settings, not a standard. Each organisation sets its own.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its wards, patient codes, days and counts. Patients appear only as anonymous codes. It records operational dates and reasons; it does not decide detention, risk, treatment or readiness to leave, and it never scores staff or wards. Coming next (Jan 2027): the live bed board for mental health, rehabilitation and long-stay beds, ready-to-leave dates recorded by the team, placement and support requests tracked by stage, and out-of-area placements with start and end dates.
