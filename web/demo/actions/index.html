Incidents, risk and actions

Anyone can report an incident or near miss from a phone in under a minute. A quality and safety lead reviews it, groups it with similar reports and sets actions. Leadership sees every action, from any source, in one tracker. Built for learning, never for blame.

Guide

The guide reads the reports, actions and risks and suggests what to look at, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear rules running in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. It only proposes: a person confirms every change.

Example General Hospital is fictional, and every report, action and risk here is invented for this demo. Owners are roles, never named people, and no one is scored.
