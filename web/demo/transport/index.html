Patient transport: book, dispatch, track

Wards book a transfer between sites on a phone and see when it is coming. The transport desk matches requests to vehicles and crews. Crews tap each step. The manager sees on-time pickups and which vehicles are off the road. The guide suggests shared journeys and flags vehicle checks; a person decides.

What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the requests, vehicles and checks, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. The transport desk or the manager decides.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its sites, vehicles, crews, patient codes and times. Patients appear only as anonymous codes, with no clinical notes. The requesting team sets urgency and escort needs; Ospeli does not set clinical priority, triage emergency calls or score drivers. Coming next (Dec 2026): daily vehicle checks on a phone, work orders sent to fleet maintenance, discharges from the bed board waiting for transport, and a journey-step delay review.
