Primary and community care: visits, clinics, follow-ups and community places

Home visit rounds and clinic lists on a phone that keeps working without a signal. Health centre managers see sessions, follow-ups, stock and referrals from the hospital in one place. The district manager compares centres as rates and sees which community places are free for patients ready to leave hospital. The guide points things out; a person decides.

    Guide

    The guide reads the visit round, the follow-up register, sessions, stock and community places, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. A named role confirms or declines it.

    Everything here is invented: the community services of Example General Hospital, its four health centres, areas, sessions and times. Patients appear only as codes. It measures visits, sessions, follow-ups and waits, never individual staff, and it does not judge clinical control, change treatment or decide who is clinically eligible for a place.
