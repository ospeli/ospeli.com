What happened

    Guide

    The guide reads the lists, booked times, recorded times and tomorrow's readiness check, and suggests where to look, with its reasons. In this preview it uses clear, checkable rules that run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. A suggestion only becomes a proposal. The theatre coordinator decides. It never decides which patient goes first or whether a patient is fit for surgery.

    Everything here is invented: Example General Hospital, its theatres, wards, lists, patient codes and times. It measures sessions, lists and waits, never individual surgeons or staff, and it does not decide the order of patients or clinical fitness. Coming next (Jan 2027): case times tapped on a phone by the theatre nurse, trays per list for sterile services, released and unused sessions over time, and the live link to beds on the bed board.
