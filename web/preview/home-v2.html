What it is Read moreClose

A tool for the people who run hospitals and clinics. It shows how a patient visit really runs, step by step.

Walking a visit comes first. Other pages cover beds, equipment and work orders, transfers, stores, clinics, diagnostics, pharmacy, cleaning and transport, facilities and patient feedback.

See all modules
How a walk works Read moreClose
  1. Follow one patient. Tap the phone at each step. Pause, undo, or mark an interruption and carry on.
  2. Read the timed map. Every step is timed and the longest wait is marked.
  3. Walk the next patient and compare. See if the same wait comes up again.
What it will not do Read moreClose
  • Make clinical decisions. People decide.
  • Send walk timings off the phone.
  • Need a network connection, or AI, to work.
